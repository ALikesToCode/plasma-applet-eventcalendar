What opens from the clock

Plasma Event Calendar replaces the panel clock. Click it to see everything coming up, gathered from the places you keep it.

  • Google Calendar

    Events from the calendars you choose, in the colours you gave them in Google Calendar. Add an event by typing a sentence like “Lunch on Friday at 1pm”, and edit or delete events from the agenda.

  • Google Tasks

    Tasks sit in the agenda next to your events. Tick them off, edit them or delete them without opening a browser.

  • Weather

    An hourly chart of temperature and rain, and a forecast beside each day in the agenda, from OpenWeatherMap or Environment Canada.

  • iCalendar feeds

    Add any .ics link, such as a school timetable or a bin collection schedule, and its events join the agenda.

  • Plasma calendars

    Public holidays and the other calendars you turn on in Plasma show up as well.

  • Clock and timer

    Write your own clock format across one or two lines, run a countdown timer with one-click presets, and see other time zones when you hover over the clock.

Install

You need Plasma 6, git and Python 3. The install script adds the widget for your user and restarts Plasma Shell, so you don't have to log out. It may ask for your password to install jq or kpackagetool6 if they're missing.

Then right-click your panel, choose Add Widgets and drag Event Calendar onto the panel. It works well in place of the Digital Clock.

  • ./update fetches the latest version and reinstalls it. It notices which Plasma you run and switches branch if it needs to.
  • ./uninstall removes the widget.

Connect Google Calendar

Sign in once from the widget's settings. Your events and tasks then appear in the agenda and stay up to date on their own.

  1. Right-click the clock and choose Configure Event Calendar, then open the Google Calendar page.

  2. Press Login. Your browser opens Google's sign-in page.

  3. Choose your account. Google lists what the widget will be able to do. Press Allow.

  4. Go back to the settings, choose the calendars and task lists to show, and press Apply.

The widget picks up your sign-in through a temporary address on your own computer, so there's nothing to copy. If your browser shows a code instead, or the settings didn't change, use the helper below.

Your calendar stays between you and Google

Plasma Event Calendar runs on your computer and has no server of its own. Your Google Calendar and Google Tasks data goes only between your computer and Google. The developer never receives it. Read the full Privacy Policy to see what the widget accesses, where it stores it and how to remove it.

Plasma Event Calendar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
